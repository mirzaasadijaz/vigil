# SAMPLE - Intrusion Alert SOP (replace with your real SOP)

## When an intrusion alert fires
1. Open the alert snapshot and confirm a person is actually in the restricted zone.
2. If the person is not wearing a visible staff badge, call the security supervisor immediately.
3. Mark the alert thumbs-up (correct) or thumbs-down (wrong) and write the reason in the note.

## False alarms
Reflections on glass, posters of people and shadows at night are the most common false intrusion
detections. Mark them thumbs-down with a short note so the false-detection layer can be tuned.

## Escalation
Critical alerts not acknowledged within 10 minutes are escalated to the security manager.
